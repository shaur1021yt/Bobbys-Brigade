Partnership

Pledge to Humanity

Bobby's Brigade partners with Pledge to Humanity to bring the community together in service of kids in need.

A shared mission

Pledge to Humanity is a community initiative Bobby's Brigade partners with each year to expand its reach beyond teddy bears — combining forces on events, donation drives, and outreach that put kindness into action for children and families facing hardship.

Together, the two organizations host community events, most notably an annual gathering timed around Cinco de Mayo, bringing volunteers, families, and CHP together in one place.

Want to get involved?

Our joint events are one of the easiest ways to volunteer alongside Bobby's Brigade in person.
