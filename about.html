Our Story

About Bobby's Brigade

How a kid's toy drive turned into a statewide comfort program riding along in CHP patrol cars.

It started with a bag of outgrown toys

Bobby's Brigade began the way a lot of good things do: small, and close to home. As a young boy in Alamo, California, Bobby started collecting toys he'd outgrown to send to children at the Open Arms Mission in Mexico — photographing each hand-painted box on its journey so the kids donating could see the joy on the other end.

That habit of noticing who needed something, and doing something about it, didn't stop there.

Then he heard the bears had run out

Through a family friend connected to the California Highway Patrol, Bobby learned that officers used to carry teddy bears in their vehicles — a small comfort for children separated from an injured parent while officers secured an accident scene. The funding for that program had dried up.

Bobby decided to bring it back himself. What began as a personal project turned into an ongoing partnership with CHP — supplying more than 18,000 care packages across California over Bobby's 13 years of service so far.

In 2018, Bobby's Brigade also supplied care packages to first-responder vehicles in five other states: Alaska, Alabama, Arkansas, Missouri, and New York. That expansion ran for one year — without the marketing reach to sustain interest in those states, the ongoing program has stayed focused on California ever since.

What's in a care package

Each $3 package includes:

• One teddy bear
• A coloring book
• Colored pencils
• A secret note from the child who packed it

Beyond teddy bears

Showing up when it mattered most

COVID-19 response

During the pandemic, Bobby's Brigade donated over 70,000 masks to CHP and 2,000 masks to Contra Costa County Health Services.

Pledge to Humanity

Bobby's Brigade partners with Pledge to Humanity on community events, including an annual gathering held around Cinco de Mayo.

The Kids Challenge

Bobby challenges kids in other states to start their own teddy bear program for their local highway patrol — reach out to get started.
